The Journey

Our vision and mission weren’t written in a single sitting. They came out of a process — part listening, part looking outward, part distillation.

01

Listened

Conversations across our founding team, drawn from very different portfolios — leadership, branding, finance, outreach — kept surfacing the same starting point: ISI Delhi trains some of the sharpest analytical minds, yet that talent rarely turns into ventures.

02

Looked outward

We studied entrepreneurship cells and incubators on other campuses, sat in on founder talks and industry conferences, and drew on lived experience in consulting, competitive programming, and family businesses to see how the strongest startup communities actually operate.

03

Distilled

The same threads kept repeating — a safe space to fail, workshops that pair business fundamentals with statistics, direct lines to mentors and investors, and a brand identity that makes entrepreneurship feel approachable rather than exclusive. Those threads became our tagline, and the four pillars of our mission.

“Measure. Model. Make impact.” — the sentence we kept coming back to.
